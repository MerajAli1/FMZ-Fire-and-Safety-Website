Which fire extinguisher do you need?

Extinguisher typeSuitable forTypical locations
Dry powder (ABC)Solids, flammable liquids, gases and electrical firesGeneral purpose — offices, factories, vehicles and warehouses
CO₂ (carbon dioxide)Electrical equipment and flammable liquidsServer rooms, electrical panels, kitchens' electrical areas, labs
Foam (AFFF)Solids (wood, paper, fabric) and flammable liquidsWorkshops, garages, fuel and chemical storage
WaterSolids such as wood, paper, fabric and plasticsOffices, schools, warehouses (not for electrical or oil fires)
Wet chemicalCooking oil and fat firesCommercial kitchens, restaurants and canteens

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which fire extinguisher is best for an office?

Most offices use dry powder (ABC) extinguishers for general areas and CO₂ extinguishers near computers and electrical panels. We recommend the exact mix after understanding your layout.

How many fire extinguishers do I need?

It depends on floor area, the type of fire risk in each area and travel distances. Share your floor plan or site details and we will recommend the number and placement.

Do you supply bulk and corporate orders?

Yes. We supply organisations of every size, from single offices to multi-site factories.

Can you train our staff to use the extinguishers?

Yes. We offer fire safety training with live extinguisher practice, so your team knows how to use the equipment you buy.
