Fire Safety Training

Training that prepares your team for a real fire

Most workplace fires are preventable — and when one does start, the first few minutes decide the outcome. FMZ's fire safety training gives your staff the knowledge and confidence to spot hazards, raise the alarm, choose and use the right extinguisher, and get everyone out safely.

Since 2014 we have trained thousands of employees across Karachi, from textile mills and pharmaceutical plants to banks, universities and schools. Every course combines short classroom modules with practical exercises, and can be delivered at your premises or ours, in Urdu or English.

What the course covers

Fire science & prevention

The fire triangle, classes of fire (A, B, C, electrical, cooking oil) and the most common causes of workplace fires.

Using fire extinguishers

Choosing the right extinguisher for each fire and using it with the PASS technique — with live practice.

Evacuation & assembly

Escape routes, assembly points, headcounts and helping visitors or people with limited mobility.

Fire warden duties

What wardens do before, during and after an incident, and how to sweep and clear an area.

Raising the alarm

Alarm systems, emergency calls and clear communication during an incident.

Hazard spotting

Housekeeping, electrical risks, safe storage of flammables and daily checks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is a fire safety training course?

A standard course usually takes one day. Refresher sessions for previously trained staff are shorter. We recommend the right format once we understand your site and the number of participants.

Can you conduct the training at our factory or office?

Yes. Most clients prefer on-site training because staff learn on their own escape routes and equipment. We can also train your team at our facility.

Do participants receive a certificate?

Yes. Every participant who completes the course receives a certificate, and you receive attendance records that are useful for compliance audits, buyers and insurers.

How often should staff be retrained?

We recommend a refresher at least once a year, training for every new joiner, and extra sessions after changes to your building, processes or emergency plan.

Is the training available in Urdu?

Yes. Courses are delivered in Urdu, English or a mix of both, depending on your team.
