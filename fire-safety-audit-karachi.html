Fire Safety Audits

Find fire hazards before they become incidents

Fire risks build up quietly: a blocked exit, an overloaded socket, an expired extinguisher, flammable stock stored in the wrong place. A fire safety audit by FMZ looks at your premises with fresh, expert eyes and identifies the hazards that could start a fire or stop people escaping.

You receive clear, prioritised recommendations, so you know what to fix first. Audits are useful before buyer or social-compliance audits, insurance renewals, after renovations, or simply to make sure your people are protected.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What happens during a fire safety audit?

We walk through your premises, inspect escape routes, equipment, electrical and storage hazards, and review your emergency procedures and training records. Afterwards you receive our findings and recommended actions.

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. A single office can be inspected quickly, while a large factory takes longer. We give you a time estimate when you book.

Will the audit help with buyer or compliance audits?

Yes. Many exporters use our audit to find and fix fire-safety gaps before social-compliance or buyer audits.

Can you help fix the problems you find?

Yes. We can supply the right equipment, train your staff and help you improve your emergency procedures.
